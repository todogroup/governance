# TODO Group Steering Committee

## Resolution 2026-03

## Status: Proposed on 2026-03-05

## Subject: Establish Working Group - "OSPO for Agents" with SC Leadership

### Proposal

The TODO Group community recognizes that AI agents and AI-driven systems are becoming increasingly prevalent in software development and organizational workflows. Organizations need guidance on establishing and managing Open Source Program Offices (OSPOs) that address the unique governance, compliance, and operational challenges posed by AI agents.

During community discussions in the #topics-oss-ai channel, there has been growing interest in developing comprehensive resources around "OSPO for Agents" including:
- **Case Studies**: Real-world examples of organizations implementing OSPO practices for AI agent governance
- **Patterns**: Common architectural and governance patterns for agent-based systems within enterprise OSPOs
- **Guidance**: Best practices and decision frameworks for OSPO leaders managing AI agent adoption
- **Reference Materials**: Technical resources, templates, and maturity models specific to agent governance

The request is for the Steering Committee to approve the creation of a new Working Group with SC leadership (at least one SC member as chair or co-chair) focused on "OSPO for Agents" to develop, curate, and maintain these resources within the TODO Group community.

### Deliverables (Phase 1)

1. **WG Charter & Scope Document** - Defining WG objectives, scope, and success metrics
2. **Case Study Collection Framework** - Template and process for collecting and curating case studies
3. **Patterns Catalog** - Document 3-5 key patterns for OSPO/agent governance
4. **Guidance Document** - Best practices guide for OSPOs managing AI agents
5. **Community Engagement Plan** - Strategy for gathering feedback and driving adoption

### Resolved

WHEREAS, the charter of the TODO Group, dated in accordance with the governance documentation, empowers the Steering Committee to approve and establish new Working Groups;

AND WHEREAS, the community has expressed clear interest in developing resources to support OSPOs in managing AI agents and agent-based systems;

BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED, that the Steering Committee approves the establishment of a new Working Group titled **"OSPO for Agents"** with the mission of developing case studies, patterns, guidance, and reference materials to help organizations establish effective governance practices for AI agents within their OSPOs.

The WG shall be led by Steering Committee members and open to all TODO Group participants, operating under the existing WG governance structure and operational principles defined in the TODO Group Charter.

## Rationale

The emergence of AI agents as a significant component of software development and enterprise workflows represents a natural evolution area for OSPO governance. This proposal aligns with TODO's mission to elevate the professional practice of OSPOs by providing actionable guidance on contemporary challenges. Establishing a dedicated WG allows the community to collaboratively develop resources that will help organizations navigate the intersection of open source policy and AI agent governance.
