# Proposal: OSPO for Agents Working Group Establishment

## Executive Summary

This proposal requests the TODO Group Steering Committee to **approve the establishment of a new Working Group focused on "OSPO for Agents"**. This WG will develop comprehensive guidance, case studies, governance patterns, and reference materials to help organizations implement effective Open Source Program Office (OSPO) practices for managing AI agents and agent-based systems.

**Status**: Ready for SC Review & Voting  
**Timeline**: 90-day Phase 1 launch plan  
**Community Interest**: High (evidenced by #topics-oss-ai discussions)

---

## The Opportunity

### Why Now?

AI agents are becoming fundamental to software development and enterprise operations. However, organizations face significant challenges:

- **Governance Uncertainty**: No clear frameworks for OSPO oversight of agent systems
- **Compliance Gaps**: Uncertainties about licensing, security, and risk management for agents
- **Lack of Patterns**: Limited real-world examples of how organizations are managing this
- **Knowledge Deficit**: OSPO practitioners seeking peers' experiences and best practices

### The Ask

We propose establishing a working group with SC leadership to:

1. **Research** - Collect case studies from real organizations managing OSPO practices for agents
2. **Develop** - Create governance frameworks, patterns, and best practices guidance
3. **Document** - Produce actionable materials for the OSPO community
4. **Share** - Publish materials and maintain as a community resource

### The Impact

Within 6 months, we aim to deliver:
- **5 published case studies** from organizations
- **7 governance patterns** with real-world examples
- **Comprehensive best practices guide** for OSPO practitioners
- **Reference materials library** (templates, checklists, decision frameworks)
- **Active community** of 25-40 engaged contributors

---

## Proposal Details

### Mission

To provide the open source community with actionable resources and best practices that enable organizations to establish governance frameworks, compliance strategies, and operational processes for AI agents within their OSPOs.

### Scope

Focus areas:
- **Governance & Compliance** - Licensing, compliance, and security for AI agents
- **Architecture Patterns** - Common patterns for integrating agents into enterprise OSPOs
- **Risk Management** - Identifying and mitigating agent-specific risks
- **Organizational Readiness** - Assessing and preparing teams for agent adoption
- **Case Studies** - Real-world examples from OSPO practitioners
- **Policy & Process** - Templates, frameworks, and decision models

**Out of Scope**:
- General AI/ML ethics (handled by other initiatives)
- Technical AI safety/alignment research
- AI policy/regulation advocacy
- Foundation model licensing strategy (beyond OSPO governance aspects)

### Leadership Structure

- **WG Chair**: SC member (assigned by Steering Committee)
- **Co-chairs**: 1-2 community volunteers approved by Chair
- **Work Stream Leads**: Volunteers leading specific deliverables
- **Members**: Open to all TODO Group participants

### Phase 1 Deliverables (6 months)

| Deliverable | Description | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Governance Framework** | Decision models, risk assessments, compliance frameworks | Month 3-4 |
| **5 Case Studies** | Real organizations sharing agent governance experiences | Month 2-4 |
| **7 Governance Patterns** | Documented patterns with examples and best practices | Month 2-3 |
| **Best Practices Guide** | Comprehensive guidance for OSPO practitioners | Month 4-5 |
| **Reference Materials** | Templates, checklists, maturity models, decision tools | Month 4-5 |
| **Community Engagement** | Regular meetings, feedback loops, recognition program | Ongoing |

See [90-DAY-ROADMAP.md](./90-DAY-ROADMAP.md) for detailed timeline.

### Success Metrics

- **Community**: 25-40 active contributors, 60%+ meeting attendance
- **Content**: 5+ case studies, 7+ patterns, 40+ page guidance document
- **Adoption**: 200+ GitHub stars, 100+ resource downloads in month 1
- **Impact**: 30+ organizations using resources within 6 months
