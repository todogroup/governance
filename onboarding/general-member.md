# General Member Onboarding

This guide should provide your organization's representatives helpful onboarding resources once your [TODO General Membership Application](https://todogroup.org/join/) has been validated and processed.

An issue should have been created to process your company's membership (via this [template](/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/onboarding-general-member.md)).

If you run into any issues during the onboarding process, please reach out to the TODO Group Program Manager ([@anajsana][todo-pm]).

## Meetings, calls, and working groups

TODO meetings and working-group activities are coordinated through the [LFX application](https://lfx.linuxfoundation.org/). Create an LFX account to see TODO meeting invitations and manage your participation; we no longer use Google Calendar for TODO meetings.

Explore the [TODO working groups on GitHub](https://github.com/todogroup/working-groups) to discover the areas where TODO operates and find the relevant repositories, discussions, channels, and calls. In particular, see the [Agentic AI to Empower OSPOs working group](https://github.com/todogroup/working-groups/tree/main/wg-agentic-ai), which focuses on practical ways to help OSPOs manage agentic AI and related open-source ecosystems.

Working groups are managed by co-chairs who moderate calls and help coordinate the work. Depending on the group, resources and ongoing work may live in dedicated GitHub repositories, such as [OSPOlogy](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology), including its issues, discussions, and working-group materials. Check each working group's GitHub page for its current scope, participation details, and meeting information.

TODO General Members may also be invited to ad-hoc, member-only touchpoints. These are not scheduled meetings; they are convened when important topics arise around technology and policy relevant to open source and AI management and the OSPO profession. Facilitators and moderators from TODO member organizations help guide these conversations. Example topics include preparing for Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) deadlines, new open protocols emerging in the agentic AI stack, open source models, and news about AI governance.

For the [TODO Group Overview Presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1p4dhx0Dg8fZDO8yzp7nWC2r5WHyVH-jjSQM59lkKLdo/edit?usp=sharing), see the membership overview deck.
