## 🎯 Key Proposal Points

### What's Being Proposed
A new TODO Group Working Group focused on **"OSPO for Agents"** to develop:
- Governance frameworks for AI agent oversight
- Case studies from real organizations
- Reusable governance patterns
- Best practices guidance
- Reference materials & templates

### Why It Matters
AI agents are increasingly prevalent in software development. Organizations need OSPO guidance specifically for agent governance, compliance, and risk management.

### Expected Timeline
- **Phase 1**: 6 months
- **Deliverables**: 5 case studies, 7 patterns, best practices guide, reference materials
- **Community**: 25-40 active contributors
- **Use**: 30+ organizations within first year

### Resources Needed
- **Budget**: None for Phase 1
- **Team**: 1 SC member (chair), 1-2 co-chairs, 25-40 volunteers
- **LF Support**: Project Manager coordination (standard)
- **Time Commitment**: Varies (can be minimal to ~10 hrs/week)
