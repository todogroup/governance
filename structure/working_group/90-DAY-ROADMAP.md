## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Low Contributor Participation
**Mitigation**:
- Targeted outreach to OSPO practitioners
- Sponsor organizations providing dedicated staff
- Clear contribution pathways
- Recognition and incentives

### Risk: Content Quality Issues
**Mitigation**:
- Peer review process with SC oversight
- SME validation of technical content
- Community feedback loops
- Multiple review rounds

### Risk: Scope Creep
**Mitigation**:
- Clear Phase 1 scope in charter
- Regular scope review in meetings
- Documented decision log
- Defer enhancements to Phase 2

### Risk: Resource Constraints
**Mitigation**:
- Realistic timelines based on volunteer capacity
- Work stream leads for distribution
- Modular work allowing parallel streams
- Flexibility to adjust if needed
