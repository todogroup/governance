# OSPO for Agents Working Group Charter

## Overview

The **OSPO for Agents Working Group** is established to develop comprehensive guidance, case studies, patterns, and reference materials to help organizations implement effective Open Source Program Office (OSPO) practices for managing AI agents and agent-based systems.

## Mission

To provide the open source community with actionable resources and best practices that enable organizations to establish governance frameworks, compliance strategies, and operational processes for AI agents within their OSPOs.

## Scope

This working group focuses on:

1. **Governance & Compliance** - How OSPOs manage licensing, compliance, and security for AI agents
2. **Architecture Patterns** - Common architectural patterns for integrating agents into enterprise systems
3. **Risk Management** - Identifying and mitigating risks specific to AI agent deployment
4. **Organizational Readiness** - Assessing and preparing organizations for agent-based workflows
5. **Case Studies** - Real-world examples of OSPO practices applied to agent systems
6. **Policy & Process** - Templates and frameworks for agent governance policies

## Goals

### Primary Goals (Phase 1: 6 months)

1. Develop a comprehensive **OSPO for Agents Charter & Framework** document
2. Create a **Case Study Collection** of 3-5 organizational case studies (write or curate)
3. Document **5-7 Key Governance Patterns** with examples and best practices
4. Publish a **Guidance Document** on OSPO practices for agent management
5. Build a **Reference Materials Repository** with templates, checklists, and tools

### Secondary Goals (Ongoing)

1. Maintain and update resources based on community feedback and evolving practices
2. Present findings at TODO Group webinars and community events
3. Engage with OSPO practitioners for insights and validation
4. Coordinate with related working groups (Metrics, Career Path, etc.) on alignment

## Operating Principles

1. **Community-Driven**: All work is developed collaboratively with community input
2. **Open Source**: All materials are contributed under the same licensing as TODO Group (Apache 2.0 / CC-BY-4.0)
3. **Inclusive**: Diverse perspectives from different organizations and roles are actively sought
4. **Evidence-Based**: Recommendations are grounded in real-world experience and case examples
5. **Transparent**: All decisions and progress are documented publicly

## Success Metrics

- **Engagement**: Minimum 10-15 active contributors per month
- **Content**: Delivery of all Phase 1 deliverables within 6-month timeline
- **Adoption**: 50+ downloads/forks of resources within first 3 months of publication
- **Community Feedback**: Positive feedback scores (4+/5) on published materials
- **Presentations**: Minimum 2 community presentations and 1 conference talk in first year
