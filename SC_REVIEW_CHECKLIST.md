## The Vote

### Voting Format

```
Resolution: OSPO for Agents Working Group Establishment

Proposed: 2026-03-05
Voting Period: [Dates to be determined]

Vote Options:
- APPROVE: Establish the WG as proposed
- DECLINE: Reject the proposal
- ABSTAIN: Do not vote
- ABSENT: Unable to participate
```

### How to Vote

1. **Timing**: SC will determine voting period (likely 1-2 weeks)
2. **Method**: Email ballot to SC members
3. **Quorum**: 5 of 7 members participating
4. **Threshold**: Simple majority (4 of 5+ votes)

### What Happens on Each Outcome

**If APPROVED** (4+ votes):
- ✅ Working group officially established
- ✅ Public announcement sent
- ✅ SC member assigned as chair
- ✅ WG begins recruitment and kickoff (Week 1)

**If DECLINED** (4+ votes against):
- ❌ WG not established (for now)
- 📋 Feedback provided to community
- 📋 Proposer can revise and resubmit

**If INCONCLUSIVE** (mixed votes):
- 📋 Discussion to address concerns
- 📊 May revote with modifications
- 📋 SC may request changes to proposal

## After the Vote

### If Approved - SC Responsibilities

- [ ] **Assign Leadership** (within 1 week)
  - [ ] Identify SC member to chair or co-chair
  - [ ] Announce chair publicly

- [ ] **Support Phase 1** (ongoing)
  - [ ] Monthly progress updates
  - [ ] Attend quarterly checkpoint meetings
  - [ ] Provide guidance on decisions
  - [ ] Help troubleshoot blockers

- [ ] **Review Deliverables** (at month 6)
  - [ ] Assess Phase 1 outputs
  - [ ] Decide on Phase 2 direction
  - [ ] Provide feedback for improvement

### If Declined - Process

- [ ] **Share Feedback**
  - [ ] SC communicates rationale to proposer
  - [ ] Document in resolution notes

- [ ] **Path Forward**
  - [ ] Proposer can request feedback session
  - [ ] Option to revise and resubmit
  - [ ] Alternative: Initiate as experimental/focus group first
